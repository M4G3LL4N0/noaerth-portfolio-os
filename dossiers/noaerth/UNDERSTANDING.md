# noaerth

## What is this?
Noaerth is the public studio site: what the studio builds, which work is featured, and how to reach the full portfolio and Labs.

## Who is it for?
Someone deciding where to start in the portfolio, not an operator running the queue.

## Problem
A wall of every startup hides what is mature, experimental, or worth opening first.

## What exists
A homepage with an intent router, a Labs teaser, featured work, and a filterable venture wall. A studio section now separates featured work, the full directory, and Labs.

## Distinctive
It is the public home of the studio. It is not the control plane and it does not list internal reviews.

## Maturity
Product

## Primary workflow
Read the studio framing, open a featured product, or go to the portfolio directory. Labs is the public progress feed.

## Incomplete
Production is behind the git main that contains the Labs teaser and the studio section.

## Opportunity
Keep homepage prominence editorial. Do not rank startups by folder order.
